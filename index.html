EVALUATION DESIGN

Three partitions. One explicit decision rule.

Training fits candidate models. Validation selects calibration-aware performance and the operating threshold. Test data is used once for the final report.

01 · COMPARE

Candidate models

Calibrated logistic regression and a class-weighted random forest compete on validation probability quality.

02 · DECIDE

Cost-aware threshold

An explicit 8:1 false-negative cost selects a threshold on validation data rather than defaulting to 0.50.

03 · MONITOR

Drift and cohorts

PSI, probability shift, permutation importance, and measurement-defined cohort slices make failure modes visible.
